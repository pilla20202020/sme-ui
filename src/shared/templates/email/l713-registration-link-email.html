Below is how to register for your online account access and login to your member dashboard.

Step 1: Member Registration

Start Registration
  • Please note online account registration is not available until your start date or thereafter.
  • In this step you will confirm information associated with your account by providing your Last Name, Last 4 of SSN, and Date of Birth.
  • Next you will be asked to verify your email and phone number via a one-time four digit code, which you will enter before hitting next.
  • Next you will be asked to create your USERNAME (Email Address) and PASSWORD (passwords are CaSe SeNsiTivE).
  • Once completed you can move on to STEP 2.

Step 2: Member Login

Login to Your Dashboard
  • If you have not completed STEP 1 you will not be able to login.
  • Please note online account access is not available until your start date or thereafter.
  • Enter your USERNAME and PASSWORD (created in STEP 1).
  • Verify your login, select your email address and submit. You will be emailed a 4 digit verification code.
  • Enter the verification code that was emailed to your Email Address and submit. (Please check your spam folder if you do not see the email.)
  • Recognize the device (Yes or No) so you won't be asked to verify every time you login from this computer/device. (If you select No, or login from a different computer/device, you will be asked to verify again.)
  • You have now logged in and can view your account, documents, and information related to your account.
